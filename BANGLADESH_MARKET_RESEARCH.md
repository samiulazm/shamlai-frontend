## Executive Summary

The Bangladesh e-commerce market is experiencing explosive growth, projected to reach **$13 billion by 2027** (up from $9 billion in 2024). With **186 million mobile connections**, **129 million internet users**, and **93 million smartphone owners**, Bangladesh represents a massive opportunity for e-commerce platforms.

---

## Market Size & Growth

- **2024 Revenue:** $6-9 billion USD
- **2027 Projected:** $13 billion USD
- **Annual Growth Rate:** 30-35%
- **Mobile Penetration:** 70%+ of online shoppers use smartphones
- **Internet Users:** 129 million (and growing)

**Effort:** MEDIUM

**Why Critical:**
- 11.33% already prefer mobile banking payments
- 60% of all transactions in Bangladesh go through bKash
- Contactless payments are the future trend

**Implementation Needed:**

## Market Risks & Considerations

### **Challenges:**
1. **Trust Issues:** E-commerce fraud concerns (Evaly scandal)
2. **Payment Security:** Users skeptical of online payments
3. **Logistics:** Inconsistent delivery in rural areas
4. **Digital Literacy:** Varying tech proficiency levels
5. **Competition:** Established players with deep pockets

### **Mitigation Strategies:**
- Emphasize security features and trust badges
- Partner with reputable payment providers
- Start with urban focus, expand gradually
- Provide excellent customer education and support
- Focus on niche markets and personalization as differentiation
